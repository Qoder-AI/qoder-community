## Use Cases

- "Research keywords for my SaaS product targeting small teams" — keyword-research turns pasted or connected data into a prioritized keyword and topic-cluster plan
- "Audit this article for E-E-A-T and publish readiness" — content-quality-auditor scores it against the 80-item CORE-EEAT benchmark with a SHIP / FIX / BLOCK verdict
- "Find TikTok creators for a skincare launch and score their fit" — influencer-discovery builds a vetted candidate pool, fit-scorer ranks it with go/pass verdicts
- "Audit my Google Ads account before I scale spend" — ad-account-auditor runs the ROAS gate on your own-account export, no ad-platform API keys needed
- "Design a win-back email campaign and check it before sending" — reactivation-specialist plans the program, email-quality-auditor gates it

## Example

Install, then describe a marketing goal in natural language — skills route themselves by context. The bundle covers four discipline lifecycles (research→build→optimize→monitor and their equivalents) plus a shared protocol layer with truth registries and cross-session memory.
